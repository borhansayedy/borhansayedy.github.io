Part 5: Track Your Income & Expenses

Before you can build a realistic budget, you need to know where your money actually goes. Record every dollar of income and every expense, every day, for about six months. At the end, total each category (you'll likely be surprised by at least one of them), then build a budget around what you find, and stick to it.
