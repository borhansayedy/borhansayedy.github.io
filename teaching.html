Teaching

I teach across the Department of Economics, Finance & Quantitative Analysis at Kennesaw State University, with a focus on making financial and economic reasoning practical for students headed into business careers. I've also taught undergraduate and graduate courses at the University of Groningen (Netherlands), where I supervised bachelor's and master's theses.

I try to build a learning experience that's rigorous but stays connected to students' everyday lives. Rather than lecturing straight through, I bring in current economic developments, short applied activities, and structured discussion so students see how the theory operates outside the classroom — especially in intro-level courses, where the relevance isn't always obvious at first.

I'm a two-time recipient of KSU's CETL Innovation in Teaching Award, and I'm an active contributor to the Bagwell Center's Financial Literacy Improvement Project (FLIP), where I help develop financial literacy workshops with colleagues in the department.

Rated 5.0/5.0 on Rate My Professors (110 ratings), with 99% of students saying they'd take my class again and a difficulty rating of 1.8/5.

Principles of Finance

Core concepts in time value of money, valuation, risk and return, and capital budgeting — the foundation for everything else in finance.

Corporate Finance

How firms make investment, financing, and payout decisions, with an emphasis on real-world capital structure and governance trade-offs.

Contemporary Economic Issues

Applying economic reasoning to current events — monetary policy, markets, and the forces shaping the economy students will graduate into.
