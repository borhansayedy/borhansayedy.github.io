Research

My work sits in applied microeconomics and household finance. One line of research looks at how individual shareholders influence corporate boards through voting behavior and ownership structure; a second grows out of the financial literacy workshops I run at KSU, examining how targeted education changes people's financial confidence and long-term financial behavior.
